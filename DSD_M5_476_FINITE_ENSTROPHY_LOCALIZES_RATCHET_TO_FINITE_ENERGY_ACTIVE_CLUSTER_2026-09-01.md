\[
S(x,t)=PV\int K_S(x-y)\Omega(y,t)dy,
\qquad
|K_S(z)|\lesssim |z|^{-3}.
\]

Let the exterior contribution be generated by `|y|>R` with `R>=2M`.

For `x in B_M`,

\[
|x-y|\ge R/2.

\[
\begin{aligned}
|S_{>R}(x,t)|
&\lesssim
\left(
\int_{|y|>R}|x-y|^{-6}dy
\right)^{1/2}
\|\Omega(t)\|_2\\
&\lesssim
R^{-3/2}\|\Omega(t)\|_2.
\end{aligned}

Thus

\[
\boxed{

\|S_{>R}(t)\|_{L^\infty(B_M)}

## 6. Localized rotational velocity has finite kinetic energy

Define the Biot--Savart velocity generated by the compact solenoidal vorticity packet:

\[
V_R^{loc}:=\mathcal B\Omega_R^{loc}.
\]

Because `Omega_R^loc` is compactly supported and divergence free,

\[
\int_{\mathbb R^3}\Omega_R^{loc}dx=0.
\]

Indeed, for each component,

\[
\nabla\cdot(x_i\Omega_R^{loc})=(\Omega_R^{loc})_i,
\]

and integration over space gives zero.

Therefore its Fourier transform satisfies

\[
\widehat\Omega_R^{loc}(\xi)=O(|\xi|)
\qquad(\xi\to0).
\]

Since

\[
\widehat V_R^{loc}(\xi)
=\frac{i\xi\times\widehat\Omega_R^{loc}(\xi)}{|\xi|^2},
\]

`V_R^loc` is bounded at zero frequency and hence

\[
\boxed{
V_R^{loc}\in L^2(\mathbb R^3).
}
\]

Also

\[
\nabla V_R^{loc}\in L^2,
\qquad
V_R^{loc}\in L^6
\]

by div--curl/Sobolev. Interpolation yields

\[
\boxed{
V_R^{loc}\in L^2\cap L^3\cap L^6.
}
\]

Thus every marked ancient ratchet event has a finite-energy, strong-`L3` rotational carrier approximating its local dynamics.
