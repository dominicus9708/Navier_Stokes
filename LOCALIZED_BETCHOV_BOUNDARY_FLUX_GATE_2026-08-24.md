## 1. Pointwise algebra

Let

\[
A=\nabla V=S+K,

\operatorname{tr}(S^3)

Since `tr S=0` in three dimensions,

\[
\operatorname{tr}(S^3)=3\det S.

## 2. Cubic velocity-gradient term is a divergence

Write

\[
T=\operatorname{tr}(A^3)
=V_{i,j}V_{j,k}V_{k,i}.
\]

Using `div V=0` and commuting derivatives, the exact identity is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
2T
&=\partial_j(V_iV_{j,k}V_{k,i})
-\partial_i(V_iV_{j,k}V_{k,j})\\
&\quad+\partial_k(V_iV_{j,i}V_{k,j}).
\end{aligned}
}
\]

Hence `T=div F` for a vector field satisfying

\[
\boxed{|F|\le C|V||\nabla V|^2.}
\]

The global Betchov relation is obtained by integrating this divergence over all space.
