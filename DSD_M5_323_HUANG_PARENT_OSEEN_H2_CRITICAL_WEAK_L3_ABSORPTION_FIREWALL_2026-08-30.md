\[

This note audits that implication.

## 2. Parent constrained Oseen evolution

Let `H(t)=U(t,s)h_s` be the constrained Oseen descendant generated by the fixed Navier–Stokes parent `u`, normalized by

\[
\|h_s\|_2=1.
\]

At the H1 level define

\[
\Gamma(t)=\|\nabla H(t)\|_2^2,
\qquad
K(t)=\|\Delta H(t)\|_2^2.
\]

The standard preterminal computation used in Huang gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac12\Gamma'(t)+\nu K(t)
= -\langle \mathbb P[(u\cdot\nabla)H],\Delta H\rangle.
}
\]

The Leray projector is L2-contractive, so it is harmless at this level.

## 3. Nonendpoint Serrin route

For `3<q<=infinity`, Huang uses

\[
\|u\|_q\,\|\nabla H\|_{r_q}\,\|\Delta H\|_2,
\qquad
\frac1q+\frac1{r_q}=\frac12,
\]

and interpolation

\[
\|\nabla H\|_{r_q}
\lesssim
\|\nabla H\|_2^{1-3/q}
\|\Delta H\|_2^{3/q}.
\]

Young's inequality then yields

\[
\boxed{
\frac12\Gamma'+\frac\nu2K
\le C_{\nu,q}\|u\|_q^{p_q}\Gamma,
\qquad
p_q=\frac{2q}{q-3}.
}
\]

Hence `u in L_t^p L_x^q`, `2/p+3/q<=1`, gives finite delayed second-order action.

This is the genuine subcritical/nonendpoint mechanism.

## 4. Endpoint weak-L3 calculation

The current quiet endpoint delivered by the proof tree is naturally

\[
\sup_t\|u(t)\|_{L^{3,\infty}}\le M
\]

with no universal smallness of `M`.

Use Lorentz Hölder and the sharp Sobolev-Lorentz embedding

\[
\|\nabla H\|_{L^{6,2}}
\lesssim
\|\Delta H\|_2.
\]

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
|\langle (u\cdot\nabla)H,\Delta H\rangle|
&\le
C\|u\|_{L^{3,\infty}}
\|\nabla H\|_{L^{6,2}}
\|\Delta H\|_2\\
&\le
C_{LS}\|u\|_{L^{3,\infty}}K.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\frac12\Gamma'
+\bigl(\nu-C_{LS}\|u\|_{L^{3,\infty}}\bigr)K
\le0.
}
\]

This is a top-order critical coefficient, not a lower-order Gronwall term.

## 5. Small endpoint corridor

If one had the strict smallness condition

\[
\boxed{
C_{LS}\sup_t\|u(t)\|_{L^{3,\infty}}<\nu,
}
\]

then the delayed Oseen H2 action is finite immediately:

\[
\int_r^{T_*}K(t)dt
\le
\frac{\Gamma(r)}{2(\nu-C_{LS}M)}<\infty.
\]

Thus small weak-L3 is a valid atom-exclusion corridor.

But the current no-`H/T` reduction gives bounded weak-L3, not a universal small bound.

## 6. Equivalent strain-form calculation

Integrating the transport term by parts gives schematically

\[
\langle (u\cdot\nabla)H,-\Delta H\rangle
=
\int \partial_k u_j\,\partial_jH_i\,\partial_kH_i.
\]

Hence another endpoint estimate is

\[
|I|
\lesssim
\|\nabla u\|_3\,\|\nabla H\|_6\,\|\nabla H\|_2
\lesssim
\|\nabla u\|_3\,K^{1/2}\Gamma^{1/2},
\]

and so

\[
\frac12\Gamma'+\frac\nu2K
\lesssim
\nu^{-1}\|\nabla u\|_3^2\Gamma.
\]

This would close if

\[
\int^{T_*}\|\nabla u(t)\|_3^2dt<\infty.
\]

However this quantity is Navier–Stokes scale invariant and is not currently known to be finite on the H/T frontier. Repeated first-hitting stages can each pay order-one critical action.

Thus this form does not bypass the endpoint obstruction.

## 7. Formation-axiom reading

The parent Oseen H2 question therefore splits into two genuinely different technical states:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal O_{sub}
:\quad
\text{a lower-order integrable coefficient exists},

versus

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal O_{crit}

\text{the parent enters the principal H2 form at critical size}.

The current large weak-L3 corridor belongs to `O_crit`.

These must not be conflated merely because both have bounded critical norms.

## 8. Axis-property reading

At the Oseen H1 level, the dangerous tensor is not merely the magnitude of vorticity. In the integrated form it is the velocity-gradient action

\[
\partial_k u_j\,\partial_jH_i\,\partial_kH_i.
\]

Thus the relevant axis decomposition is the alignment between

- the parent strain/rotation frame;
- the gradient frame of the Oseen descendant;
- the second-derivative direction providing `K`.

A future improvement could exploit cancellation or angular incoherence in this trilinear tensor rather than bounding it by the full weak-L3 norm.

No such coercive angular estimate is proved here.

## 9. Consequence for the Huang atom route

The desired contradiction

\[
\text{atom}
\Rightarrow \mathfrak R_u=\infty,
\qquad
\text{no-H/T}
\Rightarrow \mathfrak R_u<\infty

The second implication is currently valid only under an additional subcritical/Serrin or sufficiently small weak-L3 hypothesis.

Therefore the atom route reduces the affine-shield problem to a sharper endpoint operator problem, but does not yet close it.
