## 2. Mean-free shell packet and Campanato mass

Take a fixed-shape retained annulus and subtract its local mean.  Let the standard solenoidal localization be

\[
f_R=\chi_R(U-(U)_{A_R^*})-b_R.
\]

Up to the already typed transition/correction action, fixed-shape localization gives comparability

\[
\boxed{
\|f_R\|_2^2
\gtrsim
\|U-(U)_{A_R^*}\|_{L^2(A_R^{ret})}^2.

Define the retained Campanato quantity

\[
\boxed{

:=
R^{-1}
\|U-(U)_{A_R^*}\|_{L^2(A_R^{ret})}^2.

\[
\boxed{

## 3. Poincare converts Campanato escalation to gradient escalation

Because `f_R` is compactly supported in a domain of diameter comparable to `R`, Poincare gives

\[
\|f_R\|_2^2
\le
C R^2\|\nabla f_R\|_2^2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
R\|\nabla f_R\|_2^2
\gtrsim
\mathfrak C_A(R).
}
\]

Thus

\[
\mathfrak C_A(R)\to\infty
\]

forces a large scale-critical localized gradient-energy shell.

This conclusion does not require the derivative ratio `Gamma_R` to be large.  Indeed on a broad low-frequency packet both numerator and denominator may grow together.
