Work in whitened Gaussian coordinates `z in R^3`.

\[

## 2. The Gaussian mean b is fixed by A

For every divergence-free quadratic vector field,

\[
\Delta Q
=\nabla(\nabla\cdot Q)-\nabla\times(\nabla\times Q)

\]

Since a quadratic polynomial satisfies

\[

\]

\[
\boxed{

\qquad
c:=\nabla\times(Az).
}
\]

Thus the Gaussian constant subtraction is not an independent tensor parameter.

## 3. Homogeneous degree-two bracket

Define

\[

:=(Az\cdot\nabla)Q-AQ.
\]

\[
N_\omega=P+Ab.
\]

\[

\[

\[

Under `P=0`, the whole nonlinear source is constant:

\[

\]

Hence the desired zero-set implication is equivalent to

\[

\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Ac=0.
\]
