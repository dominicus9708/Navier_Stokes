## 1. Moving center and affine representative

Fix a terminal point `x_*` at time `T`.

Let `a(t)` be a moving center, and use

\[
y=x-a(t).
\]

For a positive Gaussian probability weight `gamma_t(y)` to be specified below, choose

\[
\boxed{
 a'(t)=\int \gamma_t(y)\,u(a(t)+y,t)\,dy,
}

\[
\boxed{

Since `div u=0`,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}L(t)=0.}
\]

Define the residual velocity

\[
\boxed{

=u(a(t)+y,t)-a'(t)-L(t)y.

Then exactly

\[
\boxed{
\int\gamma_t r=0,
\qquad
\int\gamma_t\nabla r=0,
\qquad
\nabla\cdot r=0.

\]

Thus `r` is the part of the local velocity field not represented by translation plus the Gaussian-weighted best affine gradient.

## 5. Weighted residual-gradient variance

Define

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B_\gamma(s)
=\int\gamma_s(y)|\nabla r(y,s)|_F^2dy.
}
\]

Because

\[
L=\int\gamma\nabla u,
\]

this is exactly the least-squares affine-gradient defect

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B_\gamma
=\int\gamma|\nabla u-L|_F^2.
}
\]

Write

\[
\nabla u=S+A,
\qquad
L=S_\gamma+A_\gamma.
\]

The symmetric and antisymmetric sectors are orthogonal, and in three dimensions

\[
|A(\omega_1)-A(\omega_2)|_F^2

Therefore exactly

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B_\gamma
=
\operatorname{Var}_\gamma(S)
+\frac12\operatorname{Var}_\gamma(\Omega),
}

where

\[
\operatorname{Var}_\gamma(S)
=\int\gamma|S-S_\gamma|_F^2

\[
\operatorname{Var}_\gamma(\Omega)

Thus the residual channel is a direct sum of

1. non-affine strain variation, and

## 6. Gaussian drift inequality

Let `Sigma` be positive definite and let `gamma` be `N(0,Sigma)`.

\[

\[

\[

\[
\boxed{

Here

The precise universal numerical constant is not needed for the route; the condition-number dependence is the important part.
