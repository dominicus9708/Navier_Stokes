## 5. Constant-axis aligned-tube identity

Let `n` be a fixed unit vector and decompose

\[
U=U_n n+U_\perp,
\qquad
\nabla= n\partial_n+\nabla_\perp.
\]

Incompressibility gives

\[
\boxed{
\partial_nU_n=-\nabla_\perp\cdot U_\perp.
}
\]

Let `rho=|Omega|` and let `phi` be a cylindrical cutoff whose transverse gradient is `n`-orthogonal. Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int\phi\rho^2 n^TSn
&=\int\phi\rho^2\partial_nU_n\\
&=-\int\phi\rho^2\nabla_\perp\cdot U_\perp\\
&=\int\rho^2U_\perp\cdot\nabla_\perp\phi
+2\int\phi\rho U_\perp\cdot\nabla_\perp\rho.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\int\phi\rho^2 n^TSn
=
\mathcal F_{\rm side}
+\mathcal T_{\rho,\perp},
}
\]

where

\[
\mathcal F_{\rm side}
=\int\rho^2U_\perp\cdot\nabla_\perp\phi
\]

is a side-shell material flux and

\[
\mathcal T_{\rho,\perp}
=2\int\phi\rho U_\perp\cdot\nabla_\perp\rho

is transverse transport across vorticity-magnitude interfaces.

The latter obeys

\[
\boxed{

\le

Thus a straight-axis stretching core is not self-contained: positive axial stretching requires side-shell turnover or transverse material motion across magnitude interfaces.
