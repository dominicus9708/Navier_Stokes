## 2. Full affine homogeneous smoothing

Let `U_-` be the terminal-normalized velocity at the previous adaptive checkpoint, and propagate it to a later time by the full linearized affine Navier--Stokes propagator.

The Kelvin-mode identity gives, on the bounded accumulated-strain branch,

\[
|\widehat U_{\rm inh}(k(t),t)|
\le
C_K
\exp\left(-c_K\nu\Delta t\,|k_-|^2\right)
|\widehat U_-(k_-)|.
\]

The Kelvin wavevector map has unit Jacobian because `tr L=0`, and `|k(t)|` is comparable to `|k_-|` up to `K`-dependent constants.

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\nabla U_{\rm inh}(t)\|_2^2
&\lesssim_K
\int |k_-|^2
 e^{-c_K\Delta t|k_-|^2}
 |\widehat U_-(k_-)|^2dk_-\\
&\lesssim_K
(\Delta t)^{-1}
\|U_-\|_2^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\|\nabla U_{\rm inh}(t)\|_2^2
\lesssim_K
\frac{E_U}{\Delta t}.
}
\]

For the adaptive previous checkpoint,

\[
\Delta t\gtrsim q,
\qquad
q=W^{1/3+2\varepsilon},
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\|\nabla U_{\rm inh}(t)\|_2^2
\lesssim_K
\frac{E_U}{q}.
}
\]

Here

\[
E_U=\|U\|_2^2
=W^{1/2}E_{\rm phys}
\]

under terminal first-hitting normalization.
