## 1. Stochastic Cauchy invariant

For a fixed terminal point `x` and terminal time `T`, let

\[
Z_s(x)
=
D_T^s(x)
\Omega(A_T^s(x),s),
\qquad s<T,
\]

be the Constantin--Iyer stochastic Cauchy invariant, with the standard choice of backward deformation matrix `D_T^s`.

For a smooth whole-space Navier--Stokes solution this is a backward martingale:

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E[Z_s(x)]=\Omega(x,T)
}
\]

independently of `s`.

The additive Brownian translation in the stochastic flow has zero derivative with respect to the terminal spatial label. Hence the deformation matrix has finite-variation evolution driven by `grad U`, while the Brownian martingale part of the product comes from the spatial derivative of the vorticity field.

Up to the harmless transpose convention in the definition of `D`, the stochastic differential has the form

\[
\boxed{
dZ_s
=
\sqrt{2\nu}\,
D_T^s\nabla\Omega(A_T^s,s)\,dW_s
}
\]

in the backward-Itô convention, with zero drift by the vorticity equation and deformation equation.

## 2. Exact quadratic-variation ledger

For the vector martingale, summing component quadratic variations gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E|Z_{s_-}|^2
-|\Omega(x,T)|^2
=
2\nu
\mathbb E\int_{s_-}^{T}
|D_T^s\nabla\Omega(A_T^s,s)|_F^2ds.
}
\]

For a scalar terminal direction `e`,

\[
Y_s=e\cdot Z_s,
\]

one similarly has

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(Y_{s_-})
=
2\nu
\mathbb E\int_{s_-}^{T}
|e^TD_T^s\nabla\Omega(A_T^s,s)|^2ds.
}
\]

Thus the variance of the stochastic Cauchy invariant is not a free probabilistic parameter. It is exactly paid by a deformation-weighted vorticity-gradient action.

## 4. Bounded-second-moment branch gives positive-probability `q` deformation

Suppose

\[

Apply the elementary second-moment lower-tail estimate to the nonnegative random variable `|Z|`:

\[
\mathbb P\left(

\frac{c_0^2}{4M_0}

\[

\[
\boxed{

Thus if the stochastic Cauchy variance stays controlled, the `q`-scale deformation cannot be confined to a vanishing-probability set of Brownian histories.
