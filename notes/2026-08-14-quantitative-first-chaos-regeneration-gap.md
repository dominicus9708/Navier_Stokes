Let `Pi_<=2` denote projection onto Hermite degrees at most two.

For scalar or fixed-component fields `f,g`,

\[
\boxed{

\|(\mathcal N+1)^{1/2}f\|_2
\|(\mathcal N+1)^{1/2}g\|_2.
}
\]

Reason: every Hermite test polynomial of degree at most two is a linear combination of

\[

\[
