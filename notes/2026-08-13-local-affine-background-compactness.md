## 2. Near/intermediate/far decomposition

Fix one tracked normalized core near the origin and a buffer radius `R>>1`.  Decompose the Biot--Savart velocity gradient schematically into

\[
\nabla U
=\nabla U_{\rm near}
+\nabla U_{\rm far}.
\]

The near part is generated by vorticity inside a fixed multiple of `B_R`; because that vorticity is bounded in every finite `Lp`, Calderon--Zygmund gives corresponding local `Lp` bounds on `grad U_near`.

For the remote part, the first-hitting `L-infinity` cap and the kernel derivative estimate give, across the unit core,

\[
\boxed{
\nabla U_{\rm far}(y,s)
=L(s)+\mathcal R_1(y,s),
}
\]

where

\[
L(s)=\nabla U_{\rm far}(0,s),
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}L(s)=0,
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal R_1(\cdot,s)\|_{L^\infty(B_1)}
\le C/R.
}
\]

The antisymmetric part of `L` is common rotation; the symmetric part is common affine strain.

\[
