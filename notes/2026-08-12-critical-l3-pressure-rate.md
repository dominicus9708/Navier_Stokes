# Critical L3 pressure-rate audit

Date: 2026-08-12

Status: **DERIVED IDENTITY + COMPUTATIONAL CHECK + FAILED-ROUTE CANDIDATE**.

## 1. Target channel

The DSD critical velocity channel is

\[
T_3(t)=\int_{\mathbb R^3}|u(x,t)|^3\,dx.
\]

For smooth rapidly decaying solutions,

\[
\frac{dT_3}{dt}+3\nu D_3=3\Pi_3,
\]

where

\[
D_3=\int\left(|u||\nabla u|^2+\frac{1}{|u|}\sum_k(u\cdot\partial_k u)^2\right)dx
\]

and

\[
\Pi_3=\int p\,u\cdot\nabla|u|\,dx.
\]

The global advection contribution cancels. The viscous contribution has a favorable sign. The pressure correlation has no sign built into this identity.

## 2. Why the original benchmark is misleadingly simple

For the single `z`-axis Gaussian double-curl seed, the pressure source

\[
Q=\sum_{i,j}\partial_i u_j\partial_j u_i
\]

is even under `z -> -z`, while `|u|` is even and

\[
u\cdot(u\cdot\nabla)u
\]

is odd. The decaying whole-space pressure generated by `Q` therefore inherits even parity, so

\[
\Pi_3=0
\]

exactly for this benchmark.

This is a symmetry cancellation and must not be interpreted as a general pressure estimate.

## 3. Breaking the symmetry

Use two smooth divergence-free components:

- the original `z`-axis seed centered at the origin;
- an `x`-axis seed translated by one unit along the `x` direction.

Two reflection-related placements were tested.

For the placement centered at `(-1,0,0)`, a deterministic spectral Poisson audit on a rapidly decaying window gives a **positive** pressure correlation. For the reflected placement centered at `(1,0,0)`, the pressure correlation has the opposite sign.

At the highest baseline resolution (`96^3`, numerical window `[-6,6)^3`) the positive configuration gives approximately

\[
T_3\approx84.8365700,
\]

\[
\Pi_3\approx6.37091894,
\]

\[
D_3\approx664.578015.
\]

The positive `Pi_3` remains positive at resolutions `48^3`, `64^3`, `80^3`, and `96^3`; its relative spread over these runs is about `0.33%`.

The reflection-related configuration gives `Pi_3<0` with almost equal magnitude.

These sign statements are **COMPUTATIONAL CHECKS**, because the pressure is recovered numerically by spectral inversion on a large decay window.

## 4. Amplitude homogeneity

For one fixed spatial shape and a positive overall amplitude `A`,

\[
T_3(Au)=A^3T_3(u),
\]

\[
D_3(Au)=A^3D_3(u),
\]

while the pressure Poisson equation implies

\[
p(Au)=A^2p(u)
\]

up to an irrelevant additive constant, hence

\[
\Pi_3(Au)=A^4\Pi_3(u).
\]

Therefore

\[
\left.\frac{dT_3}{dt}\right|_{t=0}
=3A^3\left(A\Pi_3(u)-\nu D_3(u)\right).
\]

For the positive-pressure numerical shape with `nu=1`, the computed crossover amplitude is approximately

\[
A_*\approx104.31.
\]

At `A=120`, the numerical balance predicts a positive initial `T_3` derivative.

This is strong computational evidence against a proof strategy that assumes unconditional monotone decay of the global `L^3` channel. It is **not yet a rigorous whole-space counterexample theorem**, because the nonzero `Pi_3` value itself has so far been established numerically rather than by certified interval or closed-form integration.

## 5. DSD interpretation

The critical channel must retain at least

\[
(T_3,D_3,\Pi_3)
\]

as distinct typed entries. Collapsing them into a single positive scalar loses the mechanism that can feed or drain the critical velocity norm.

In a multi-component state, the off-diagonal interaction channels found in the translation/coupling audit feed the pressure source and therefore influence `Pi_3`.

## 6. Route status

### Retained route

Seek a non-circular bound of the form

\[
\left|\Pi_3\right|
\le
\theta\nu D_3+F(\text{already controlled critical channels}),
\qquad 0\le\theta<1,
\]

or another estimate strong enough to keep `T_3` bounded.

This is **CONJECTURE / TARGET**; no such closing estimate has been proved here.

### Failed-route candidate

The shortcut

\[
\frac{d}{dt}T_3\le0
\quad\text{for all smooth data}
\]

is marked **FAILED-ROUTE CANDIDATE / COMPUTATIONAL COUNTEREXAMPLE** pending rigorous certification of the asymmetric pressure integral.

## 7. Numerical interpretation boundary

The FFT domain is only a numerical quadrature/Poisson-inversion window for rapidly decaying whole-space data. It is not being interpreted as a physical cube, container, or periodic version of the proof problem.

Before promoting the failed-route candidate to a theorem-level negative result, the pressure integral should be independently certified by either:

1. a closed-form/spherical-harmonic or Gaussian-polynomial inversion;
2. rigorous interval quadrature with a proven far-field error bound; or
3. an equivalent analytic construction with a manifestly signed `Pi_3`.
