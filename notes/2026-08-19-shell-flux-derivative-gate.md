# Large-radius Betchov shell flux forces enstrophy or derivative growth

Date: 2026-08-19

Status: **DERIVED LOCAL FUNCTIONAL ESTIMATE / T-TO-H OR NON-TIGHT ENSTROPHY REDUCTION / GLOBAL REGULARITY NOT PROVED**.

This note sharpens the `T` branch generated by the exact local Betchov flux.

---

## 1. Shell flux

For a cutoff `phi_R` adapted to a ball of radius `R`, with

\[
|\nabla\phi_R|\lesssim R^{-1},
\]

and a local translation velocity `c`, the Betchov shell term obeys

\[
|\mathcal F_{B,R}|
\lesssim
R^{-1}
\int_{A_R}|u-c|\,|\nabla u|^2dx,
\]

where `A_R` is the cutoff annulus.

Choose `c` as a local mean on a slightly enlarged ball so that Sobolev--Poincare gives

\[
\|u-c\|_{L^6(A_R)}
\lesssim
\|\nabla u\|_{L^2(B_{2R})}.
\]

---

## 2. Interpolation of the gradient factor

Holder with exponents `6,12/5,12/5` gives

\[
\int_{A_R}|u-c||\nabla u|^2
\le
\|u-c\|_6
\|\nabla u\|_{12/5}^2.
\]

Interpolate

\[
\|\nabla u\|_{12/5}
\le
\|\nabla u\|_2^{3/4}
\|\nabla u\|_6^{1/4}.
\]

A local Sobolev estimate on `B_{2R}` gives

\[
\|\nabla u\|_6
\lesssim
\|\nabla^2u\|_2
+R^{-1}\|\nabla u\|_2.
\]

Define

\[
E_R=\int_{B_{2R}}|\nabla u|^2dx,
\qquad
P_R=\int_{B_{2R}}|\nabla^2u|^2dx.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
|\mathcal F_{B,R}|
\lesssim
R^{-1}E_R^{5/4}P_R^{1/4}
+R^{-3/2}E_R^{3/2}.
}
\]

The second-derivative channel is equivalent, modulo fixed whole-space Fourier constants, to a palinstrophy-type channel.

---

## 3. Order-one distant flux cannot stay cheap

Suppose

\[
|\mathcal F_{B,R}|
\ge F_0>0.
\]

Then at least one of the two terms above is order `F0`, so either

\[
\boxed{
E_R^5P_R
\gtrsim
F_0^4R^4,
}
\]

or

\[
\boxed{
E_R
\gtrsim
F_0^{2/3}R.
}
\]

Thus for a sequence `R -> infinity`, an order-one shell flux forces one of:

1. derivative/palinstrophy growth (`H`);
2. enstrophy growth at least linearly in the shell radius;
3. both.

Under the normalized first-hitting cap `||Omega||_infty <= 1`, divergent normalized enstrophy is a spatial non-tightness certificate.

Therefore a distant shell cannot transport an order-one Betchov production imbalance while both enstrophy and palinstrophy remain uniformly bounded.

The previous near-saturation power gain showed that, if the Betchov shell flux is subdominant,

\[
\delta_\phi\to0
\Longrightarrow
H\text{ or spatial non-tightness}.
\]

The estimate here shows that if the shell-flux alternative itself moves to radii `R -> infinity`, then maintaining order-one flux again forces

\[
\boxed{

}
\]

Hence large-radius `T` is not a derivative-free escape route.
