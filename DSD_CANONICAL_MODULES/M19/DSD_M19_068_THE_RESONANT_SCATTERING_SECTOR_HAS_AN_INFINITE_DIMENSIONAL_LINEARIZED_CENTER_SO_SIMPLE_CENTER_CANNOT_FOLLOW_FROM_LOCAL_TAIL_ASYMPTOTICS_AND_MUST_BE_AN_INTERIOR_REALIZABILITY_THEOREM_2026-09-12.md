\[
\text{time translation is the only center direction}

The scattering representation makes the time-translation tangent explicit.  This module asks whether the **leading critical tail itself** has only that one neutral direction.

## 3. Linearized leading deformation

Perturb the leading datum by

\[
A\mapsto A+\varepsilon B.
\]

Then

\[
\delta U_0
:=
\left.\frac d{d\varepsilon}\right|_{\varepsilon=0}
\left[r^{-1}(A+\varepsilon B)\right]

\boxed{r^{-1}B(q,\omega)}.

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{sim}\delta U_0=0

for every admissible \(B\).

Thus every admissible scattering perturbation is a neutral mode of the leading similarity transport.

## 4. Divergence-free constraint still leaves infinitely many neutral modes

Write

\[

and decompose the tangential part on \(S^2\):

\[

=\nabla_{S^2}\phi_B

The leading divergence-free condition is the same as in M19-041:

\[
\boxed{

Hence the free data are again two scalar channels, a mean-zero radial scalar and a toroidal scalar.

In particular the pure toroidal family

\[
\boxed{

Choosing \(c\) from any infinite-dimensional bounded function class produces an infinite-dimensional neutral subspace.
