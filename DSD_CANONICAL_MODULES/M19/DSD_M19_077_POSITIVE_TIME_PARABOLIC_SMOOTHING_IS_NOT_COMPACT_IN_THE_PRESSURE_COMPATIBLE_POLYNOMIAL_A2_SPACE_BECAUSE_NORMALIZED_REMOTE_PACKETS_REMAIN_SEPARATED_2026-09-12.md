## 2. Free similarity semigroup

Consider the scalar/vector free similarity generator

\[
\boxed{
L_0
=\nu\Delta
-\frac12y\cdot\nabla
-\frac12.
}
\]

The corresponding equation is

\[
\partial_t f=L_0f.
\]

Set

\[
x=e^{-t/2}y,
\qquad
\tau=1-e^{-t}.
\]

A direct change of variables gives the exact semigroup formula

\[
\boxed{
(S_0(t)f_0)(y)
=e^{-t/2}
\left(e^{\nu(1-e^{-t})\Delta}f_0\right)(e^{-t/2}y).
}
\]

Thus diffusion smooths the packet, while the similarity drift moves a feature initially near radius \(R\) to radius

\[
\boxed{R(t)\sim e^{t/2}R.}
\]

This is the same outward similarity conveyor seen in the scattering calculation.

## 3. Normalized translated packets

Choose a nonzero smooth compactly supported test packet \(\phi\) supported near the origin.
For the divergence-free vector problem one may choose \(\phi\) divergence free.

Define

\[
f_R(y)=c_R\phi(y-Re_1),
\]

where \(c_R\) is chosen so that

\[
\boxed{\|f_R\|_{L^2(w_a)}=1.}
\]

Since

\[
w_a(Re_1+z)
\sim C R^{-a}
\]

uniformly for bounded \(z\),

\[
\boxed{c_R\asymp R^{a/2}.}
\]

The family \(f_R\) is bounded but has no strongly convergent subsequence as \(R\to\infty\).

## 4. Weighted norm after positive time

Let

\[
H_t:=e^{\nu(1-e^{-t})\Delta}.
\]

Then

\[
S_0(t)f_R(y)
=e^{-t/2}c_R
(H_t\phi)(e^{-t/2}y-Re_1).
\]

Compute the weighted norm using \(y=e^{t/2}x\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|S_0(t)f_R\|_{L^2(w_a)}^2
&=
 e^{t/2}c_R^2
\int
|H_t\phi(x-Re_1)|^2
w_a(e^{t/2}x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}
\]

On the packet region \(x=Re_1+O(1)\),

\[
w_a(e^{t/2}x)
\sim
 e^{-at/2}w_a(x).
\]

Using the normalization of \(c_R\), one obtains for each fixed \(t>0\)

\[
\boxed{
\|S_0(t)f_R\|_{L^2(w_a)}^2
\longrightarrow
C_{\phi,t}
 e^{(1-a)t/2},
}
\]

where

\[
C_{\phi,t}>0.
\]

In particular,

\[
\boxed{
\liminf_{R\to\infty}
\|S_0(t)f_R\|_{L^2(w_a)}>0.
}
\]

The spectral gap damps the norm by a fixed factor, but does not send remote normalized packets to zero at any fixed positive time.

## 5. Pairwise separation survives smoothing

Take \(R_n\to\infty\) with

\[
|R_n-R_m|\to\infty
\quad(n\neq m)
\]

fast enough.

At time \(t>0\), the packet centers are approximately

\[
e^{t/2}R_ne_1.
\]

Their widths depend on \(t\) and \(\phi\), but not on \(R_n\).

The Gaussian tails generated by \(H_t\) have exponentially small overlap between sufficiently separated centers. Therefore

\[
\langle S_0(t)f_{R_n},S_0(t)f_{R_m}\rangle_{L^2(w_a)}
\to0
\]

for \(n\neq m\) along a separated subsequence.

Since every output has a uniform positive norm floor,

\[
\boxed{
\{S_0(t)f_{R_n}\}
\text{ has no strongly convergent subsequence.}
}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
S_0(t):L^2(w_a)\to L^2(w_a)
\text{ is not compact for any fixed }t>0.
}
\]

## 6. Why ordinary Rellich smoothing does not contradict this

On every fixed bounded ball,

\[
H^1(B_R)\hookrightarrow L^2(B_R)
\]

is compact.

The failure is entirely global:

\[
\boxed{
\text{local smoothing/Rellich compactness}
+\text{no uniform tail tightness}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{global compactness}.
}
\]

The polynomial A2 norm permits unit vectors to escape to larger and larger radii by increasing their amplitude like \(R^{a/2}\).

This is exactly the same representation defect as the q-translation sequence in M19-072, expressed in physical similarity radius.

## 7. Extension to the quiet linearized Navier--Stokes cocycle

On the controlled weak-critical spectator branch,

\[
|U(y,\theta)|=O(|y|^{-1}),
\qquad
|\nabla U(y,\theta)|=O(|y|^{-2})
\]

in the retained remote norm.

A perturbation packet centered at radius \(R\) therefore sees the linearized transport/stretching coefficients with size

\[
O(R^{-1})+O(R^{-2})
\]

over any fixed similarity-time interval before lower-order corrections are included.

The pressure correction is generated by a weighted Calderón--Zygmund operator applied to products such as

\[
U\otimes W+W\otimes U.
\]

Because \(w_a\in A_2\), that pressure operator is bounded in the same weighted space, while multiplication by the remote background is small on the translated packet.

Hence the full linearized cocycle over a fixed time is asymptotically the free similarity semigroup on packets translated sufficiently far into the spectator region.

Schematically,

\[
\boxed{
S_U(t)f_R
=S_0(t)f_R+o_{R\to\infty}(1)
\quad\text{in }L^2(w_a).
}
\]

Therefore the same separated-packet sequence prevents compactness of the quiet full cocycle.

This statement uses the spectator coefficient bounds already required for the scattering normal form; failure of those bounds is a previously typed remote/derivative exit.
