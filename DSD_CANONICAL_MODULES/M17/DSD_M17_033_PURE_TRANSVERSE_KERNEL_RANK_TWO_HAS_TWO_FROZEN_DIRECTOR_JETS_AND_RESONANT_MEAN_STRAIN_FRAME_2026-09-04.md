\[

\[

=(D_Bn\cdot\nabla)\xi

\[

\[

For the gradient commutator,

\[
D_B(\nabla\xi)
=-(\nabla B)^T\text{ acting on the derivative slot of }\nabla\xi.

\[

\[

\boxed{
