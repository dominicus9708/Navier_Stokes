## 1. Goal

M17-449 separates the dimensional Poincare constant as

\[
C_P=\mathfrak A\Pi,
\]

where `mathfrak A` is normalized transverse area and `Pi` is scale-free shape degeneration.

The present module asks whether growth of `mathfrak A` is merely an optional escape or is already forced by bounded enstrophy on a parent-length positive-flux loop.

## 2. Uniform enstrophy on fixed normalized record windows

Use the record scaling

\[
\Omega_R(y,s)=R^2\Omega(Ry,R^2s).
\]

Then

\[
\|\Omega_R(s)\|_2^2
=R\|\Omega(R^2s)\|_2^2.
\]

M5-475 gives the first-generation Type-I enstrophy decay

\[
\|\Omega(t)\|_2^2
\lesssim(-t)^{-1/2}
\]

for large negative `t`.

Therefore on every fixed compact normalized time window

\[
I\Subset(-\infty,0)
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{s\in I}\|\Omega_R(s)\|_2^2
\le E_I<\infty
}
\]

uniformly for large record factor `R`.

## 3. Coherent vortex-tube geometry

At one normalized time, let `Gamma_R` be a retained coherent vortex loop with

\[
\boxed{
\ell_R:=\operatorname{length}(\Gamma_R)
\ge c_\ell R.
}
\]

Let `A_z` denote a transverse cross-section along arclength `z`.

Assume the retained tube chart has bounded Jacobian comparability so tube integrals are equivalent, up to fixed constants, to

\[
\int_{\Gamma_R}\int_{A_z}(\cdots)\,dA\,dz.
\]

Because the side boundary is tangent to vorticity and `div Omega_R=0`, the oriented positive vorticity flux through coherent cross-sections is independent of `z` at the fixed time. Denote it by

\[
\Phi_R\ge\Phi_*>0.
\]

Define

\[
Q_R(z):=\int_{A_z}|\Omega_R|^2dA,
\qquad
\mathfrak A_R(z):=|A_z|.
\]

## 4. Flux versus cross-sectional enstrophy

Cauchy--Schwarz gives at every cross-section

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_R^2
\le
\mathfrak A_R(z)Q_R(z).
}
\]

Hence

\[
Q_R(z)
\ge
\frac{\Phi_R^2}{\mathfrak A_R(z)}.
\]

The tube enstrophy satisfies

\[
E_{tube,R}
\asymp
\int_{\Gamma_R}Q_R(z)dz
\le
\|\Omega_R\|_2^2
\le E_I.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_R^2
\int_{\Gamma_R}
\frac{dz}{\mathfrak A_R(z)}
\lesssim E_I.
}
\]

## 5. Harmonic-mean area lower bound

Define the arclength harmonic mean

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak A_{H,R}
:=
\left(
\frac1{\ell_R}
\int_{\Gamma_R}\frac{dz}{\mathfrak A_R(z)}
\right)^{-1}.
}
\]

Section 4 gives

\[
\mathfrak A_{H,R}
\gtrsim
\frac{\Phi_R^2\ell_R}{E_I}.
\]

Using `Phi_R >= Phi_*` and `ell_R >= c_l R`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak A_{H,R}
\ge c_A R.
}
\]

This is the central theorem.

## 6. Flux-weighted amplitude interpretation

At each section define the M17-440 quadratic currency

\[
\mathfrak Q_\Phi(z)=Q_R(z)
\]

in record-normalized own-scale units and

\[
\mathfrak a_\Phi(z):=\frac{Q_R(z)}{\Phi_R}.
\]

Its loop average obeys

\[
\overline{\mathfrak a}_{\Phi,R}
:=
\frac1{\ell_R}
\int_{\Gamma_R}\mathfrak a_\Phi(z)dz
\lesssim
\frac{E_I}{\Phi_R\ell_R}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\overline{\mathfrak a}_{\Phi,R}
\lesssim
\frac{C}{R}.
}
\]

Thus parent-length positive flux under uniform record enstrophy automatically forces average flux-weighted normalized amplitude collapse at least at record-linear rate.

This is stronger than treating amplitude collapse as a wholly optional qualitative escape.

## 7. Natural transverse participation scale

Define the harmonic-mean participation radius

\[
\Lambda_{\perp,H}(R)
:=\mathfrak A_{H,R}^{1/2}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda_{\perp,H}(R)
\gtrsim R^{1/2}.
}
\]

Therefore the positive-flux tube is forced to occupy at least `sqrt(R)` transverse own-scales in harmonic-mean area sense.

It cannot remain uniformly own-scale-thick while its loop length remains parent-order and its total record enstrophy stays bounded.

## 8. Correction to M17-448 interpretation

A regular approximately isotropic cross-section with area

\[

Thus the record-linear `C_P` threshold found in M17-448 can be generated by ordinary enstrophy-forced transverse broadening and need not represent a thin neck.

This confirms the necessity of the M17-449 size/shape correction.

## 9. What is and is not closed

M17-450 proves a baseline broadening theorem, not a contradiction.

The survivor can still have

\[

The new point is that the baseline factor

\[
