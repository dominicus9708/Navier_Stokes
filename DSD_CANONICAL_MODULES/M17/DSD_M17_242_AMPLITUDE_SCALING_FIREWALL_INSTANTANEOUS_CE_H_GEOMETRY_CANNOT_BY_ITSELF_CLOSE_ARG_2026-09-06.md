## 1. CE-H amplitude scaling

Fix one nonzero instantaneous CE-H field satisfying

\[
\boxed{
\Delta W=\kappa W.
}
\]

For any \(\varepsilon>0\), define the algebraically rescaled field

\[
\boxed{W_\varepsilon:=\varepsilon W.}
\]

Then

\[
\Delta W_\varepsilon
=\varepsilon\Delta W
=\kappa\varepsilon W
=\kappa W_\varepsilon.
\]

Hence the same multiplier \(\kappa\) is retained:

\[
\boxed{\kappa_\varepsilon=\kappa.}
\]

This is an algebraic scaling test of the instantaneous CE-H relation. It is not claimed that every \(W_\varepsilon\) is a member of the fully normalized Navier--Stokes recurrent hull.

## 2. Relative geometry is unchanged

Where \(W\neq0\), the director is

\[
\xi=\frac{W}{|W|}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\xi_\varepsilon=\xi.
\]

Likewise

\[
\nabla\kappa_\varepsilon=\nabla\kappa,
\qquad
\Delta\kappa_\varepsilon=\Delta\kappa.
\]

Thus quantities such as

\[
\ell^2\kappa,
\quad
\ell^3\nabla\kappa,
\quad
\int_{B_\ell}|\kappa|^{3/2},
\quad
\ell\|\nabla\kappa\|_{L^{3/2}},
\]

are unchanged by the amplitude rescaling.

The same is true for director anisotropy and other purely relative orientation descriptors.

## 3. Physical quadratic costs collapse with amplitude

The enstrophy mass scales as

\[
\boxed{
\int|W_\varepsilon|^2
=\varepsilon^2\int|W|^2.
}
\]

The vorticity palinstrophy scales as

\[
\boxed{
\int|\nabla W_\varepsilon|^2
=\varepsilon^2\int|\nabla W|^2.
}
\]

The multiplier-diffusion charge scales as

\[
\boxed{
\int |W_\varepsilon|^2|\nabla\kappa|^2
=\varepsilon^2
\int |W|^2|\nabla\kappa|^2.
}
\]

Thus any lower bound of the schematic form

\[
\text{relative kappa geometry}
\Longrightarrow
\int\rho^2|\nabla\kappa|^2\ge c_*>0

cannot follow from the homogeneous CE-H relation alone without an amplitude-normalizing hypothesis.

## 4. Physical flux is only linear in amplitude

For any fixed material or geometric surface \(\Sigma\),

\[
\Phi_\varepsilon
:=\int_\Sigma W_\varepsilon\cdot n\,dA
=\varepsilon\Phi.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{|\Phi_\varepsilon|=\varepsilon|\Phi|.}
\]

This is the algebraic reason behind the M17-241 vanishing-flux firewall.

No purely instantaneous unweighted kappa descriptor can quantize the physical vortex flux.

## 7. Full similarity dynamics is not amplitude homogeneous

Write the similarity vorticity equation as

\[
\partial_\theta W
+\frac12 y\cdot\nabla W
-\Delta W
+W
+
\Big(U[W]\cdot\nabla W-\Sigma[W]W\Big)
=0,
\]

with \(U[W]\) and \(\Sigma[W]\) linear in \(W\) through Biot--Savart.

Define the linear part \(\mathcal L W\) and quadratic part \(\mathcal N(W,W)\).

Then

\[
\mathcal L(\varepsilon W)
=\varepsilon\mathcal LW,
\]

while

\[
\mathcal N(\varepsilon W,\varepsilon W)
=\varepsilon^2\mathcal N(W,W).
\]

Therefore the full equation is not invariant under arbitrary amplitude scaling.

This is exactly where an amplitude-return theorem may enter.
