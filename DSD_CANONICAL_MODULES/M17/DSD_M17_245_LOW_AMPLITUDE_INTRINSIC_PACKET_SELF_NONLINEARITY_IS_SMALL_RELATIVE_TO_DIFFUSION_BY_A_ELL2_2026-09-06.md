## 1. Intrinsic packet normalization

Let \(B_\ell\) be a coherent intrinsic packet with radius \(O(\ell)\).

Assume the non-amplitude-concentration branch, so on the retained good bulk

\[
|W|\lesssim a
\]

with mean amplitude scale \(a>0\).

The packet enstrophy mass is

\[
\boxed{M\asymp a^2\ell^3.}
\]

At its own intrinsic second-derivative scale,

\[
\boxed{
\|\Delta W\|_{L^2(B_\ell)}
\asymp M^{1/2}\ell^{-2}.
}
\]

If the selected raw ratio is even larger than this scale-comparable form, retain strict subscale descent/re-extraction rather than using the estimate as an equality.

## 2. Local Biot--Savart velocity scale

Let \(W_{loc}=\chi_\ell W\) with a fixed-factor packet cutoff and let \(U_{loc}\) be its Biot--Savart velocity contribution.

Using the kernel size \(|K(x)|\lesssim|x|^{-2}\) and \(|W_{loc}|\lesssim a\) on a region of diameter \(O(\ell)\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
|U_{loc}(x)|
&\lesssim
\int_{|x-y|\lesssim\ell}
\frac{a}{|x-y|^2}dy\\
&\lesssim a\ell.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{\|U_{loc}\|_\infty\lesssim a\ell.}
\]

## 3. Local strain L2 scale

The strain is a Calderon--Zygmund transform of vorticity. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\|\Sigma_{loc}\|_2

\lesssim M^{1/2}.

## 4. First-derivative interpolation

For a compactly localized packet field, Fourier interpolation gives

\[
\|\nabla W\|_2^2
\le
\|W\|_2\|\Delta W\|_2.

At the own intrinsic scale,

\[
\boxed{

## 5. Self-advection is small relative to diffusion

The local self-advection satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|U_{loc}\cdot\nabla W\|_2
&\le
\|U_{loc}\|_\infty\|\nabla W\|_2\\
&\lesssim
(a\ell)(M^{1/2}\ell^{-1})\\
&\lesssim aM^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Divide by the diffusive size

\[
\|\Delta W\|_2
\asymp M^{1/2}\ell^{-2}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\|U_{loc}\cdot\nabla W\|_2}
{\|\Delta W\|_2}
\lesssim a\ell^2.
}
\]

## 6. Self-stretching is small relative to diffusion

Using \(|W|\lesssim a\) on the coherent retained packet,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\Sigma_{loc}W\|_2
&\le
\|W\|_\infty\|\Sigma_{loc}\|_2\\
&\lesssim aM^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\|\Sigma_{loc}W\|_2}
{\|\Delta W\|_2}
\lesssim a\ell^2.
}
\]

The same dimensionless parameter controls both local quadratic mechanisms.

## 8. Linear similarity terms at the intrinsic scale

The zeroth-order reaction \(-W\) has relative size

\[
\frac{\|W\|_2}{\|\Delta W\|_2}

Thus the local leading operator at the tiny scale is diffusion unless ambient/nonlocal coefficients enter at critical strength.

## 11. Heat-tangent firewall

The estimate above does not by itself produce a nonzero heat tangent.

A heat-limit argument would still require

1. scale-comparable support and an upper as well as lower normalized H2 bound;
2. nonzero normalized mass persistence;
3. enough backward lifetime or a one-sided coercive substitute;
4. control of ambient/nonlocal forcing and cutoff/interface terms.

M17-232 validates nested physical re-extraction but does not guarantee that every selected child's support radius is uniformly comparable to its **own** final intrinsic ratio; a still smaller raw subcarrier may remain.

Therefore no heat-Liouville conclusion is claimed here.
