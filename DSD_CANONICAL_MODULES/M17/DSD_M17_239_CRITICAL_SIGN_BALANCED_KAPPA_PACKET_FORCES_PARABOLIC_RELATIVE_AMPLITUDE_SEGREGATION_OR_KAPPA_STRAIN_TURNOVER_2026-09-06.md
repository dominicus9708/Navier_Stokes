## 4. Kappa-turnover action

\[

<\frac{a_*}{2}\ell^{-2}

Then by the fundamental theorem along material trajectories,

\[
\int_0^{\tau_\ell}
|D_B\kappa(\Phi_s(a),s)|ds
\ge
\frac{a_*}{2}\ell^{-2}.
\]

The same statement holds for negative turnover trajectories.

If a fixed fraction of either initial population turns over, integration over material labels and use of the exact Jacobian gives

\[

\[
