## 4. The leading positive sheath is l=3-neutral

The quadratic polynomial `F_2` contains only spherical-harmonic degrees

\[
\ell=0,2.
\]

Equivalently, it is inversion-even.

Every degree-three spherical harmonic is inversion-odd:

\[
Y_{3m}(-\omega)=-Y_{3m}(\omega).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_{\ell=3}F_2=0.
}
\]

Hence the leading local density responsible for the positive nodal sheath in M17-012 has no cubic/octupole component.

This is true regardless of the anisotropy of `C=A^TA`.
