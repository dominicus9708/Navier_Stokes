## 4. Transport derivative of the diffusion level density

Set

\[
f:=m|\nabla\kappa|.
\]

Then

\[
A(k,\theta):=A_{\kappa\kappa}(k,\theta)
=
\int_{\Sigma_k}f\,dS,
\qquad
\Sigma_k:=\{\kappa=k\}.
\]

Under the level flow generated by `X`, the surface transport formula gives

\[
\frac d{dk}A(k,\theta)
=
\int_{\Sigma_k}
\left(X\cdot\nabla f+f\,\operatorname{div}_{\Sigma_k}X\right)dS.

The compact tube bounds imply

\[
|X\cdot\nabla f|
\le C_1,

|\operatorname{div}_{\Sigma_k}X|

More usefully, because

\[

\[
\boxed{

Therefore

\[
\boxed{

\le C_{tube}A(k,\theta).
}

No analyticity theorem is needed for this regular-tube estimate.

## 5. Gronwall thickening in kappa-space

For `|k|<=delta_0`, Gronwall gives

\[
\boxed{
A(k,\theta)
\ge e^{-C_{tube}|k|}A(0,\theta).
}
\]

Hence for every fixed

\[
0<\delta\le\delta_0,
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{-\delta}^{\delta}A(k,\theta)dk
&\ge
A(0,\theta)
\int_{-\delta}^{\delta}e^{-C_{tube}|k|}dk\\
&=
\frac{2}{C_{tube}}\left(1-e^{-C_{tube}\delta}\right)A(0,\theta).
\end{aligned}
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{
c_{thick}(\delta)
:=
\frac{2}{C_{tube}}\left(1-e^{-C_{tube}\delta}\right)>0.
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\int_{-\delta}^{\delta}A_{\kappa\kappa}(k,\theta)dk
\ge
c_{thick}(\delta)A_{\kappa\kappa}(0,\theta).
}
\]
