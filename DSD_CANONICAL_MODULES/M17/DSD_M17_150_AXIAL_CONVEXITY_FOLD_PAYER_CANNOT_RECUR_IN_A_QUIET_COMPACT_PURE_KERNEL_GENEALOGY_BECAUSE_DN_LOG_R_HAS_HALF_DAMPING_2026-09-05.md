## 4. Exact `n`-direction scalar commutator

For a scalar `f`, the transverse frame dynamics give

\[
D_B(D_nf)
=(D_Bn)\cdot\nabla f
+n\cdot\left(\nabla D_Bf-(\nabla B)^T\nabla f\right).
\]

M17-033 gives

\[
(\nabla B)n
=(\beta_\Sigma-r_W)k
+\left(\sigma_n+\frac12\right)n.

Since

\[
D_Bn=-(\beta_\Sigma+r_W)k,

the antisymmetric rotation cancels and we obtain

\[
\boxed{
D_B(D_nf)
=
D_n(D_Bf)
-2\beta_\Sigma D_kf
-\left(\sigma_n+\frac12\right)D_nf.
}
\]

This is the exact `n`-direction analogue of the earlier `k` and `xi` commutators.
