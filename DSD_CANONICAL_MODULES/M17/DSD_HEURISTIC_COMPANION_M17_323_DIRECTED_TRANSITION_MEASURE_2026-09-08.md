## Heuristic

When a structural transition current is signed, net cancellation can hide repeated turnover.  For audit purposes, separate the two directed transition measures before asking for a finite resource.

Applied to the zero-\(\kappa\) material current density \(j_k(0,l,\theta)\), this suggests

\[
A_-(\theta)=\int(-j_k)_+\,dl,
\qquad
A_+(\theta)=\int(j_k)_+\,dl.
\]

Then

\[
G(0,\theta)=A_+(\theta)-A_-(\theta).
\]

The canonical module proves all consequences from this elementary identity and the previously established current law.  DSD theory contributes no additional PDE assumption.
