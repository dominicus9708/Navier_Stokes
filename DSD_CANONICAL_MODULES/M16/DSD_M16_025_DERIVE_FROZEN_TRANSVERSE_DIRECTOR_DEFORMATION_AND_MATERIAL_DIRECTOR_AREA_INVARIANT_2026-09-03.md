## 2. Material-vector version

Let `v(theta)` be any material line element satisfying

\[

\[
\begin{aligned}
D_B\big((v\cdot\nabla)\xi\big)

\end{aligned}

\[
\boxed{

\[

\[
D_B(v\cdot\xi)

\[
\boxed{

So material transverse line elements remain transverse to the material vortex direction.
