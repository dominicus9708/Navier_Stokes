# External-Audit Import Rules R28–R38

Date: 2026-09-07

These rules extend the M17 external-audit regression suite after the final citation-role classification of the 16 deep-audit families.

## R28 — Failure-as-evidence firewall

A failed or superseded external global proof may be cited positively only for statements that survive independently of the failed hinge.
The failed hinge itself may be used only as `ANTI_LESSON` evidence against the corresponding inference pattern.

## R29 — Partial-survivor isolation

Never import an entire paper because one lemma survives.
Pin the exact lemma, hypothesis, version and output used by M17.
A failed global conclusion does not invalidate a correct local identity; a correct local identity does not validate the global conclusion.

## R30 — Version-pinning rule

Every rapidly revised external proof program must be cited by exact version/date/record.
If a later version explicitly identifies a missing hypothesis or barrier, earlier unconditional wording is treated as superseded for DSD evidentiary purposes.

## R31 — Representation-equivalence rule

An auxiliary field, lift, scheduler, projection API, controller or host-space representation may constrain classical NSE only after an exact bidirectional equivalence/conjugacy theorem is proved.
No representation layer may add admissibility conditions that prune classical trajectories.

## R32 — Compactness-is-not-regularity rule

Smooth approximants plus weak/energy compactness do not export smoothness to the limit.
Any regularity upgrade must carry a uniform estimate in a continuation/regularity norm through the approximation limit.

## R33 — Physicalization before accumulation

Every normalized local payment must be translated to physical variables before it is summed against a global budget.
The proof must retain the exact scale/amplitude weight and prove nonsummability or a multiplicity compensation theorem.

## R34 — Geometry/amplitude separation

Any director, topology, Crofton, Morse, entropy or orientation quantity invariant under `omega -> epsilon omega` is amplitude-blind.
No amplitude-blind geometric quantum may be exported as a fixed physical dissipation quantum without a PDE-derived amplitude bridge.

## R35 — State-dependent weight ledger

For every weight `w=w(u,p,omega,...)`, retain the complete transport/diffusion derivative, including `(D_t + nu Delta)w` and all integration-by-parts commutators.
A positive/increasing weight is not free extra viscosity.

## R36 — Moving-frame inverse-gap rule

Whenever a spectral projector/eigenvector frame is differentiated, price the inverse eigen-gap and retain the small-gap set as an explicit branch unless an independent integrable sublevel theorem is proved.
Analytic smoothing alone is not uniform transversality.

## R37 — Selected-scale quantifier rule

Existence of one good scale does not imply control of every finer scale.
Approximate-identity, endpoint and terminal-transfer steps must preserve quantifier order and constants on the same physical window.

## R38 — Coverage theorem for finite ledgers

A finite branch/carrier ledger is a proof architecture, not a closure theorem.
Before claiming exhaustion, prove that every possible compactness defect, interface leak, scale escape, genealogy transition, coefficient exit and residual term enters one priced class with controlled overlap and no unlisted remainder.
