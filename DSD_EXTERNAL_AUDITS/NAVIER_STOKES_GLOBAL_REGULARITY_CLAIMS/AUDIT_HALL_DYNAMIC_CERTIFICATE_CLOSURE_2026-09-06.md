### A. Coverage
Every possible loss of compactness/regularity arising from a suitable weak Navier–Stokes singularity must map to at least one listed carrier.

### B. Faithful routing
Every routing rule between carriers must follow from the NSE/local-energy/pressure equations with uniform constants and cannot discard a residual during normalization, gauge choice, collar passage, or tail limit.

Internal termination of a finite graph does not itself prove A or B.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{

\Rightarrow
\text{PDE contradiction}.
}
\]

The exhaustiveness/faithfulness clauses are genuine mathematical theorems, not bookkeeping conventions.
