# DSD Audit — Recognition-Science Eight-Beat / Universal Angular Sector Claim

Date: 2026-09-06
Source: *Nsp*, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15683951, Jun 2025.
Audit status: **EXTERNAL AXIOM / MODEL-EQUIVALENCE FAILURE IN PUBLIC CLAIM**

## 1. Public mechanism

The abstract claims unconditional global regularity and a universal vorticity bound by combining Constantin–Fefferman geometric depletion with an “eight-beat framework.” It states that the universe forces vorticity into eight angular sectors and that misaligned states decay by a fixed factor over eight cosmic ticks, producing a universal geometric constant.

## 2. DSD source-of-law audit

The classical NSE contains:

\[
\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta u,
\qquad \nabla\cdot u=0.
\]

There is no eight-beat clock, eight-sector angular constraint, golden-ratio decay law, or “cosmic tick” parameter in the equation.

To use such a rule in an unconditional proof, one must derive it as a theorem from the NSE for every admissible smooth datum.

Otherwise the logical structure is

\[
\boxed{
\text{NSE} + \text{extra physical axiom}
\Rightarrow
\text{regularity under the augmented model},
}
\]

not regularity of the original NSE alone.

## 3. Geometric depletion distinction

Constantin–Fefferman-type theorems give conditional regularity under quantitative coherence/alignment assumptions. They do not prove that every NSE solution automatically satisfies one universal angular sectorization with one fixed positive margin.

Therefore the key missing bridge is

\[
\text{arbitrary NSE trajectory}
\Rightarrow
\text{eight-sector/misalignment decay law}.
\]

A numerical or cosmological motivation cannot replace that PDE theorem.

## 4. Dimensional/universality audit

A bound of the form

\[
\|\omega(t)\|_\infty\le C_*/\sqrt\nu
\]

for arbitrary smooth initial data is also immediately suspect at `t=0`: scaling the initial vorticity amplitude by a large finite factor while keeping viscosity fixed can violate any data-independent finite bound at the initial time.

Unless the claimed bound is intended only after a data-dependent positive time or includes initial-data dependence, a universal constant independent of the initial amplitude cannot hold for arbitrary smooth data.

## 5. DSD verdict

Two independent public-level problems are present:

1. the decisive eight-beat/angular law is an external postulate rather than a demonstrated NSE consequence;
2. the stated universal vorticity ceiling is incompatible with arbitrary-amplitude initial data if it includes `t=0`.

\[
\boxed{\text{The public unconditional Clay claim is not established.}}
\]

Any conditional geometric-depletion theorem under an explicitly assumed angular law should be retained separately as a conditional model result.

Global regularity remains unproved.
