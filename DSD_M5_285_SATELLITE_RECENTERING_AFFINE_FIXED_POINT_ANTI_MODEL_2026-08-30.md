## 1. Exact affine stationary solution

Let

\[
A^T=-A,
\qquad A\ne0,
\]

and define

\[
\boxed{
u(x,t)=Ax.}
\]

Then

\[
\nabla\cdot u=0,
\qquad
\Delta u=0,
\]

and

\[
(u\cdot\nabla)u=A^2x.
\]

Because `A^2` is symmetric, the pressure

\[
p(x)=-\frac12x^TA^2x
\]

satisfies

\[
A^2x+\nabla p=0.
\]

Thus `u=Ax` is an exact stationary Navier--Stokes solution on all of

\[
\mathbb R^3\times(-\infty,\infty).
\]

Its vorticity is a nonzero constant vector.

\[

After this normalization,

\[
