## 1. Published weak-`L^{3,infinity}` solution structure

Barker--Seregin--Sverak define a weak `L^{3,infinity}` solution with initial datum

\[
u_0\in L^{3,\infty}(\mathbb R^3)
\]

by writing

\[
\boxed{
v=V+u,
\qquad
V(t)=e^{t\Delta}u_0,
}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
u\in L^\infty(0,T;L^2)
\cap
L^2(0,T;\dot H^1).
}
\]

The correction satisfies the corresponding perturbed Navier--Stokes equation, weak continuity, a global energy inequality, and a local energy inequality.

Their stability theorem assumes a sequence of initial data bounded in the **same critical norm**:

\[
\boxed{
\sup_k\|u_0^{(k)}\|_{L^{3,\infty}}
\le M<\infty.
}
\]

Weak-* convergence of the data then yields subsequential convergence of the corresponding weak `L^{3,infinity}` solutions.

Thus the published stability mechanism is stronger than local suitable compactness.

## 2. What a satellite prelimit has at one restart time

Fix a negative satellite time `sigma_0<0`.

Every prelimit satellite field

\[
\widetilde u_n(\cdot,\sigma_0)
\]

comes from a smooth finite-energy physical solution.

Hence, for each fixed `n`, it belongs to many classical spaces, in particular

\[
\widetilde u_n(\sigma_0)
\in L^2\cap L^\infty
\subset L^{3,\infty}.
\]

However the scale-invariant weak-`L^3` norm is unchanged by Navier--Stokes scaling and translation.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|\widetilde u_n(\sigma_0)\|_{L^{3,\infty}}
=
\|u(t_{n,\sigma_0})\|_{L^{3,\infty}}.
}
\]

There is no current uniform bound on the right side in the `H/T -> satellite` branch.

Indeed M5-278 shows that on a nontrivial ancient branch satisfying the Albritton--Barker terminal condition, bounded weak-`L^3` backward subsequences are forbidden; the critical norm must eventually escalate.

Thus the implication

\[
\text{smooth finite-energy prelimit data}
\Rightarrow
\text{uniform weak-}L^3\text{ restart data}
\]

is RED.

## 3. Local truncation produces a datum but not coherence

Choose a satellite-centered cutoff at radius `R` and perform the usual Bogovskii correction:

\[
\boxed{
a_{n,R}
=\chi_R\widetilde u_n-b_{n,R},
\qquad
\nabla\cdot a_{n,R}=0.
}
\]

Because `a_{n,R}` is compactly supported and the satellite solution is smooth on the fixed ball, one has

\[
a_{n,R}\in L^{3,\infty}
\]

with a finite norm depending on the retained local bounds and `R`.

One may therefore construct a global weak-`L^{3,\infty}` solution launched from `a_{n,R}`.

But this new global solution is **not automatically the original satellite solution inside `B_{R/2}`**.

The difference sees:

- pressure generated by the discarded exterior field;
- harmonic/affine velocity-gradient components generated by remote vorticity;
- material and nonlinear flux through the cutoff annulus;
- the Bogovskii/localization correction.

These are exactly the mechanisms already isolated in the localized packet equation.

Thus truncation proves existence of a convenient comparison solution, not restart coherence.

## 5. Why fixed-window convergence is insufficient

For each fixed `R`, the cutoff comparison depends only on the field inside a finite parent region plus the far-field pressure/harmonic contribution.

But to obtain the published weak-`L^3` restart structure for the **detached limit**, one must send

\[
R\to\infty.
\]

That is exactly the same order-of-limits problem isolated in M5-283:

\[
\boxed{
\text{fixed }R\text{ local convergence}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{uniform control as }R\to\infty.
}
\]

A hidden affine or sparse far-field profile may be invisible on every fixed window and still destroy global weak-critical restart coherence.

## 6. Uniform weak-`L^3` norm is a separate requirement

Even if the restart-coherence defect vanishes, Barker--Seregin--Sverak stability requires a uniform critical bound on the restart data.

Therefore one needs a backward sequence `sigma_k -> -infinity` and radii `R_k -> infinity` such that

\[
\boxed{
\sup_k
\|a_{k,R_k}\|_{L^{3,\infty}}
<\infty
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak D_{restart}(k,R_k)\to0.
}
\]

These are logically independent:

1. **critical datum bound**;
2. **coherence of the actual evolution with that datum**.

Neither follows merely from bounded satellite vorticity.
