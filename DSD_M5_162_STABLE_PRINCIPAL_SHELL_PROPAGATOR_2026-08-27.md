## 3. Kernel mass is `O(z^2)`

Since `0<zeta<=z`,

\[
\frac1\zeta-\frac1z
=\frac{z-\zeta}{z\zeta}
\ge
\frac{z-\zeta}{z^2}.
\]

Also `(zeta/z)^{1/2}<=1`.

Therefore

\[
G(z,\zeta)
\le
\exp\left[-\frac{z-\zeta}{4\nu z^2}\right].
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^zG(z,\zeta)d\zeta
\le
4\nu z^2.
}
\]

Thus the stable fast-normal channel samples a `z`-layer of parabolic thickness `O(z^2)` and exactly cancels the apparent `z^-2` singularity in `F_z=S/(4nu z^2)`.
