## 1. Purpose

The affine anti-models constructed earlier are exact local Navier--Stokes solutions with nonzero constant strain and highly coherent vorticity direction.

However, they have non-decaying affine velocity and are not finite-energy whole-space solutions.

For a finite-energy decaying whole-space solution, the strain is generated by the vorticity through Biot--Savart. The vorticity-direction representation therefore provides an additional geometric restriction that the abstract affine model does not contain.

## 2. Exact angular representation of stretching

Let

\[
 \xi(x)=\frac{\omega(x)}{|\omega(x)|}
\]

and

\[
 \gamma(x)=\xi(x)^TS[\omega](x)\xi(x).
\]

The Constantin--Fefferman / Beirao-da-Veiga--Berselli representation has the form

\[
 \boxed{
 \gamma(x)
 =
 \frac{3}{4\pi}\operatorname{p.v.}
 \int_{\mathbb R^3}
 D(\hat y,\xi(x+y),\xi(x))
 \frac{|\omega(x+y)|}{|y|^3}\,dy,
 }
\]

where

\[
 D(v_1,v_2,v_3)
 =(v_1\cdot v_3)\det(v_1,v_2,v_3).
\]

Hence

\[
 |D(\hat y,\xi(x+y),\xi(x))|
 \le
 |\xi(x+y)\times\xi(x)|
 =\sin\theta(x,x+y).
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 |\gamma(x)|
 \le
 C\int
 \frac{|\omega(x+y)|\sin\theta(x,x+y)}{|y|^3}\,dy,
 }
\]

with the usual principal-value/localization interpretation.

Most importantly,

\[
 \boxed{
 \xi(x+y)=\pm\xi(x)
 \Longrightarrow
 D=0.
 }
\]

Perfectly aligned or anti-aligned vorticity does not contribute to longitudinal stretching through this angular kernel.
