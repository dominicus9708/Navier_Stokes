## 4. Near/far strain split at the satellite center

At time `sigma=0`, write the strain at the satellite center using the Calderon--Zygmund kernel:

\[
\widetilde S_n(0)
=
\operatorname{p.v.}
\int K(z)\widetilde\omega_n(-z)dz.
\]

Split at radius one:

\[
\widetilde S_n(0)
=
S_{near,n}+S_{far,n}.
\]

The mean-zero kernel cancellation and the local Lipschitz bound give

\[
\boxed{
|S_{near,n}|
\lesssim C_{sat}.
}
\]

For the far part, Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
|S_{far,n}|
&\lesssim
\int_{|z|\ge1}
|z|^{-3}|\widetilde\omega_n(-z)|dz\\
&\lesssim
\|\widetilde\omega_n(0)\|_{L^2(\mathbb R^3)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
|\widetilde S_n(0)|
\le
C\left(
C_{sat}
+
\|\widetilde\omega_n(0)\|_2
\right).
}
\]

Every prelimit field has finite `L2` vorticity because it comes from a smooth positive-time finite-energy Navier--Stokes solution; uniformity is the issue.

## 9. What iteration does not prove

An arbitrarily long or infinite satellite chain is not presently contradictory.

The successive centers and natural scales are defined in different recentered/rescaled frames. One must not assume without proof that

\[
\ell^{(m+1)}<\ell^{(m)}
\]

in one common physical normalization, nor that the packets are simultaneously disjoint or carry additive energy.

Thus no infinite-descent, packing, or tree-width contradiction is claimed.

The gain is classification:

\[
H_{ambient}
\]

is no longer an untyped pointwise endpoint; it is another remote-scale-generation mechanism.
