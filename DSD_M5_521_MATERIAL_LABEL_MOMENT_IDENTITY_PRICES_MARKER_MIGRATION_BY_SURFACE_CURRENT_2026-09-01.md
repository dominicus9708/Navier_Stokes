## 1. Input from M5-520

On a smooth material surface `Sigma(theta)` transported by

\[
B=U+\frac12y,
\]

the signed flux density

\[

satisfies

\[
\boxed{
D_Bf+(1-\sigma_n)f+\operatorname{div}_{\Sigma}J_{\Sigma}=0,
}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
J_{\Sigma}:=(\nabla\times W)\times n.
}
\]

The material surface measure satisfies

\[
D_B(dA)=(1-\sigma_n)dA.
\]

Thus the signed measure

\[
\mu_\theta:=f\,dA
\]

has an exact surface continuity law with current `J_Sigma`.

## 3. Exact weak moment identity

Define the signed material-label moment

\[
\boxed{
M_\psi(\theta)
:=
\int_{\Sigma(\theta)}
\psi f\,dA.
}
\]

The material surface transport theorem gives

\[
\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi
=
\int_{\Sigma(\theta)}
\left[
D_B(\psi f)+(1-\sigma_n)\psi f
\right]dA.
\]

Because `D_B psi=0`, this becomes

\[
\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi
=
\int_{\Sigma}
\psi
\left[D_Bf+(1-\sigma_n)f\right]dA.
\]

Use M5-520:

\[
\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi
=
-\int_{\Sigma}
\psi\operatorname{div}_{\Sigma}J_{\Sigma}\,dA.
\]

Surface integration by parts gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi
=
\int_{\Sigma}
\nabla_{\Sigma}\psi\cdot J_{\Sigma}\,dA
-
\oint_{\partial\Sigma}
\psi J_{\Sigma}\cdot\mu\,ds.
}
\]

On the zero-boundary/compact-support test class,

\[
\boxed{
\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi
=
\int_{\Sigma}
\nabla_{\Sigma}\psi\cdot J_{\Sigma}\,dA.
}
\]

This is the exact material-coordinate weak continuity identity.

Therefore changes in `M_psi` cannot be explained by simply moving the entire material surface through similarity space.

They represent genuine redistribution of flux density relative to the material labeling.

This removes the ambiguity identified in M5-518 between

## 5. Migration lower bound

Fix a time interval

\[
I=[\theta_0,\theta_1].
\]

Integrating the weak identity,

\[
M_\psi(\theta_1)-M_\psi(\theta_0)
=
\int_I\int_{\Sigma(\theta)}
\nabla_{\Sigma}\psi\cdot J_{\Sigma}\,dA\,d\theta.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
|\Delta M_\psi|
\le
L_\psi
\int_I\int_{\Sigma(\theta)}
|J_{\Sigma}|\,dA\,d\theta,
}
\]

where

\[
L_\psi
:=
\sup_{I,\Sigma(\theta)}
|\nabla_{\Sigma}\psi|.
\]

Hence whenever

\[
|\Delta M_\psi|\ge m_0>0,
\]

we have

\[
\boxed{
\int_I\int_{\Sigma(\theta)}
|J_{\Sigma}|\,dA\,d\theta
\ge
\frac{m_0}{L_\psi}.
}
\]

Thus a fixed material-label redistribution costs fixed positive `L1` surface-current action.

## 10. Why this still does not close recurrence

`M_psi` is bounded on a compact finite-flux lineage.

Therefore recurrence allows

\[
M_\psi(\theta)
\]

to oscillate indefinitely.

The exact weak identity may have

\[
\left\langle\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi\right\rangle=0
\]

while

\[
\left\langle
\left|\frac d{d\theta}M_\psi\right|
\right\rangle>0.
\]

Equivalently, a fixed amount of signed flux may move back and forth between material-label regions while the current repeatedly reverses.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{migration price}
\ne
\text{strict drift}.
}
\]

M5-521 converts marker migration into a costed recurrent channel but does not contradict recurrence.
