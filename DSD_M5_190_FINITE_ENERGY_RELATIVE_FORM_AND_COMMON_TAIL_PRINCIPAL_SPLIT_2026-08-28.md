## 2. Whole-space relative equation as an energy-form equation

In reverse physical time `tau=T_*-t`, write schematically

\[
\partial_\tau W+\nu\Delta W
+\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(U_1\otimes W+W\otimes U_2)=0,
\qquad \nabla\cdot W=0.
\]

For divergence-free `phi in H1`, the Leray projection disappears from the dual pairing:

\[
\langle \mathbb P\nabla\cdot F,\phi\rangle
=
\langle \nabla\cdot F,\phi\rangle.
\]

Thus

\[
\left|
\langle \mathbb P\nabla\cdot(U\otimes W),\phi\rangle
\right|
\le
\|UW\|_2\,\|\nabla\phi\|_2.
\]

Using Lorentz Hölder and Sobolev,

\[
\|UW\|_2
\le
C\|U\|_{L^{3,\infty}}\|W\|_{L^{6,2}}
\le
C\|U\|_{L^{3,\infty}}\|\nabla W\|_2.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(U\otimes W)\|_{H^{-1}}
\le
C\|U\|_{L^{3,\infty}}\|\nabla W\|_2.
}
\]

At this form level, pressure is not an additional derivative loss.

## 5. Strong-L3 quotient is infinitesimally form-bounded

Consider the stretching correction generated by a strong quotient `Q in L3`:

\[
\mathfrak q_Q[W]
:=
\int W^T S_Q W\,dx.
\]

Using `div W=0`, integration by parts rewrites it schematically as

\[
\mathfrak q_Q[W]
=
-\int Q\cdot(W\cdot\nabla W)dx.
\]

For one fixed `Q in L3`, split

\[
Q=Q_{\le M}+Q_{>M}.
\]

The bounded part gives

\[
\left|
\int Q_{\le M}\cdot(W\cdot\nabla W)
\right|
\le
C_M\|W\|_2\|\nabla W\|_2
\le
\varepsilon\|\nabla W\|_2^2+C_{\varepsilon,M}\|W\|_2^2.
\]

The high-amplitude part obeys

\[
\left|
\int Q_{>M}\cdot(W\cdot\nabla W)
\right|
\le
C\|Q_{>M}\|_3\|\nabla W\|_2^2.
\]

Uniform equiintegrability of the compact strong-`L3` quotient class lets `M` be chosen uniformly so that the latter coefficient is arbitrarily small.

Consequently, uniformly over the fiber,

\[
\boxed{
|\mathfrak q_Q[W]|
\le
\varepsilon\|\nabla W\|_2^2
+C_\varepsilon\|W\|_2^2.
}
\]

This recovers the M5-128 form bound in the present backward-uniqueness language.

## 8. Important non-implication

Hardy boundedness alone does **not** prove sectoriality or backward uniqueness.

If

\[
4C_T\ge\nu,
\]

the naive energy quadratic form need not be coercive.

Adding a scalar `L2` shift does not automatically repair a supercritical inverse-square negative form because the defect is scale-critical.

Therefore the implication

\[
\boxed{
|S_{B_T}|\lesssim |x|^{-2}
\Rightarrow
\mathcal L_{B_T}\text{ sectorial/injective}
}
\]

is RED without an additional NSE-specific sign, adapted form, or Carleman argument.

This firewall prevents importing the normalized frozen semigroup result M5-178 into physical `L2` without proof.
