## 2. Rotated self-similarity is a compact recurrent Leray motion

A backwards RSS solution has the form, in standard Leray variables,

\[
V(Y,s)
=
R(\alpha s)
U(R(-\alpha s)Y)
\]

for one fixed spatial profile `U` and constant angular speed `alpha`.

Because the rotation group is compact,

\[
\{V(s):s\in\mathbb R\}
\]

is a compact orbit in every rotation-invariant Sobolev topology in which `U` lies.

For `alpha!=0`, the orbit is periodic with period

\[
\boxed{T_\alpha=2\pi/|\alpha|}
\]

(up to smaller periods from profile symmetry).

Hence RSS is a special case of recurrent Leray motion.
