## 1. Exact point-source strain kernel

Start from the Biot--Savart representation

\[
U(x)
=\frac1{4\pi}\int
W(z)\times\frac{x-z}{|x-z|^3}\,dz.
\]

For one source vector `v` at displacement

\[
r=x-z,
\qquad
n=\frac r{|r|},
\]

the symmetric part of the velocity gradient has, up to the universal signed constant, the form

\[
\boxed{
K_S(r)v
=c_S|r|^{-3}
\left[
(n\times v)\otimes n
+n\otimes(n\times v)
\right].
}
\]

The isotropic derivative term from differentiating `r/|r|^3` is antisymmetric in the velocity gradient and cancels in the symmetric strain.

The exact value/sign of `c_S` is irrelevant for the geometric identities below.

---

## 2. Radial normal strain vanishes identically

Contract the tensor twice with `n`:

\[
\begin{aligned}
n^T K_S(r)v\,n
&=c_S|r|^{-3}
\left[
(n\cdot(n\times v))(n\cdot n)
+(n\cdot n)(n\times v\cdot n)
\right]\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
n^T K_S(r)v\,n=0.}
\]

A point vorticity source produces pure shear relative to its own source--target radial direction; it does not directly stretch or compress that radius.

---

## 3. The same source can stretch another target direction

For a unit target direction `a`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
a^TK_S(r)v\,a
&=2c_S|r|^{-3}
(a\cdot n)
(a\cdot(n\times v)).
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
a^TK_S(r)v\,a
\propto
(a\cdot n)\det(a,n,v).}
\]

This is the same directional triple-product structure used in the M5-454/M5-554 stretching audit.

It may be strictly positive while

\[
n^TK_S(r)v\,n=0.
\]

Therefore the positive M5-553 cross-payer edge and the M5-554 connector compression are not the same leading kernel mode.

## 4. Finite coherent packet estimate

Let a coherent source packet be supported in

\[
B_\rho(z_b),
\]

and let the target point be `x_a`.

Set

\[
d:=|x_a-z_b|,
\qquad
n:=\frac{x_a-z_b}{d}.
\]

Assume

\[
\rho\le\frac d2.
\]

For every source point

\[
z=z_b+\eta,
\qquad
|\eta|\le\rho,
\]

we have

\[
n^TK_S(dn)W(z)n=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
|n^TK_S(dn-\eta)W(z)n|
&=
|n^T[K_S(dn-\eta)-K_S(dn)]W(z)n|\\
&\le
C\frac{|\eta|}{d^4}|W(z)|.
\end{aligned}
\]

Integrating gives

\[
\boxed{
|n^T\Sigma_{packet}(x_a)n|
\le
C\frac{\rho}{d^4}
\|W\|_{L^1(B_\rho(z_b))}.
}
\]

Thus a geometrically thin cross-payer packet has only a higher-order radial-normal strain relative to its center connector.
