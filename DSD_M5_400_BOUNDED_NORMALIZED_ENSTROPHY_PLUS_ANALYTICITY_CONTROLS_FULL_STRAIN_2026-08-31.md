## 3. Calderon--Zygmund kernel split

The strain has the principal-value representation

\[
\Sigma_j(y)
=
\operatorname{p.v.}
\int_{\mathbb R^3}
K(z)\Omega_j(y-z)\,dz,
\]

where `K` is a mean-zero matrix kernel homogeneous of degree `-3`.

Fix a radius `rho>0` and split

\[
\Sigma_j
=
\Sigma_{near}
+
\Sigma_{far}.
\]

For the near part, use the mean-zero cancellation of the kernel:

\[
\Sigma_{near}(y)
=
\operatorname{p.v.}
\int_{|z|<\rho}
K(z)
[\Omega_j(y-z)-\Omega_j(y)]\,dz.
\]

The Lipschitz bound gives

\[
|\Omega_j(y-z)-\Omega_j(y)|
\le
\|\nabla\Omega_j\|_\infty |z|.
\]

Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
|\Sigma_{near}(y)|
&\lesssim
\|\nabla\Omega_j\|_\infty
\int_{|z|<\rho}|z|^{-2}dz\\
&\lesssim
\rho\|\nabla\Omega_j\|_\infty.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|\Sigma_{near}\|_\infty
\lesssim
\rho C_1.
}
\]

## 4. The entire far field is controlled by normalized enstrophy

For the far part,

\[
|\Sigma_{far}(y)|
\lesssim
\int_{|z|\ge\rho}
\frac{|\Omega_j(y-z)|}{|z|^3}dz.
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
|\Sigma_{far}(y)|
&\lesssim
\|\Omega_j\|_2
\left(
\int_{|z|\ge\rho}|z|^{-6}dz
\right)^{1/2}\\
&\lesssim
\rho^{-3/2}\|\Omega_j\|_2.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\|\Sigma_{far}\|_\infty
\lesssim
\rho^{-3/2}Z_j^{1/2}.
}
\]

This estimate includes every remote source, including source configurations that appear harmonic/affine inside the local observation ball.

No separate pointwise harmonic-strain escape remains when `Z_j` is bounded.

## 5. Full strain estimate

Combining the two pieces,

\[
\boxed{
\|\Sigma_j\|_\infty
\lesssim
\rho C_1
+
\rho^{-3/2}Z_j^{1/2}.
}
\]

Choosing the fixed natural value `rho=1` yields the simple uniform estimate

\[
\boxed{
\|\Sigma_j\|_\infty
\le
C\left(C_1+Z_j^{1/2}\right).

\]

Therefore, if

\[
\sup_j\sup_{t\in I_j}Z_j(t)\le Z_+<\infty,

\[
\boxed{

\le

\]

## 8. Important limitation: critical strain action may still diverge

The estimate

\[
\|\Sigma_j\|_\infty\le C
\]

per normalized stage does **not** imply that the total physical critical strain action is finite.

A hypothetical singular first-hitting tower can pay an order-one scale-invariant strain action on infinitely many generations.

Thus one may still have

\[
\int^{T_*}\|S_u(t)\|_3^2dt=\infty
\]

without any single normalized parent stage having unbounded pointwise strain.

M5-400 therefore removes a pointwise/exposure interpretation of `H_nonlocal strain`; it does not close the nonsummable critical-strain frontier of M5-330--344.
