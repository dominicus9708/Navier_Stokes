## 1. Purpose

The axis decomposition `gamma,tau,D_perp` is meaningful where the vorticity direction is nondegenerate. This note separates that active region from ambient strain living where vorticity is small.

## 2. Active/ambient split

On a normalized first-hitting stage, let

\[
\|\Omega\|_\infty\le1.
\]

Fix `0<eta<1` and define

\[
A_\eta(t)=\{x:|\Omega(x,t)|\ge\eta\},
\qquad
B_\eta(t)=\{x:|\Omega(x,t)|<\eta\}.
\]

Set

\[
a(t)=\|S\|_{L^3(A_\eta(t))},
\qquad
b(t)=\|S\|_{L^3(B_\eta(t))}.
\]

Since the regions are disjoint,

\[
\|S\|_3^3=a^3+b^3.
\]

Using concavity of `x^(2/3)`,

\[
\boxed{
\|S\|_3^2
\le a^2+b^2.
}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\int\|S\|_3^2=\infty
\Longrightarrow
\int a^2=\infty
\quad\text{or}\quad
\int b^2=\infty.
}
\]

Thus active and ambient strain are an exhaustive finite partition of the critical action.

## 3. Active region: exact axis decomposition is nondegenerate

On `A_eta`, define

\[
\xi=\Omega/|\Omega|.
\]

Then

\[
|S|^2=\frac32\gamma^2+2|\tau|^2+|D_\perp|^2,
\]

with

\[
\gamma=\xi^TS\xi,
\qquad
\tau=(I-\xi\otimes\xi)S\xi.
\]

Hence nonsummable active strain action forces at least one of the active channels

\[
H_{\gamma,act},
\qquad
H_{\tau,act},
\qquad
H_{D,act}.
\]

Because `|Omega|>=eta`, the exact vorticity-direction equation

\[
(\partial_t+u\cdot\nabla)\xi
=\tau+
\frac{\nu}{|\Omega|}
(I-\xi\otimes\xi)\Delta\Omega
\]

has no small-denominator problem beyond the fixed factor `eta^{-1}`.

Thus the active tilt channel routes to

\[
\boxed{
\text{material/projective axis action}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{directional-diffusion higher-derivative action}.
}
\]

Similarly the magnitude equation

\[
(\partial_t+u\cdot\nabla)\log|\Omega|
=\gamma+
\nu\frac{\xi\cdot\Delta\Omega}{|\Omega|}
\]

shows that active longitudinal stretching can be neutralized only by directional diffusion or by repeated entry/exit of material through the active threshold, the latter being a turnover event.

These are routing statements; no global coercive line-integral theorem is asserted here.

## 4. Active transverse shear

The active `D_perp` channel does not directly change vorticity magnitude or direction. The exact affine anti-model proves this firewall.

However the transverse covariance identity already derived in the repository has the form

\[
E_\perp'=2q_\perp D_\perp+\mathcal R_\perp.
\]

On an active packet with positive transverse covariance radius, persistent coherent `D_perp` therefore routes to

- packet-shape deformation;
- transverse-strain direction turnover;
- residual/cutoff/material action;
- or loss of coherent packet structure.

All are existing H/T/covariance lanes.

## 5. Ambient region requires a local buffer audit

Now suppose the nonsummable action is carried by `B_eta`, where local vorticity is small.

Fix a ball `B_r(x0)` contained in the ambient region and choose a cutoff supported in `B_{2r}`. Split

\[
S=S_{near}+S_{far}.
\]

The near part is the Calderon–Zygmund strain generated by localized vorticity in `B_{2r}`, together with cutoff/Bogovskii corrections.

The far part is generated by vorticity outside the buffer and is harmonic/real analytic inside `B_r`.

## 6. Near ambient strain

If the entire buffer satisfies

\[
|\Omega|\le2\eta,
\]

then the principal near term obeys

\[
\|S_{near}\|_{L^3(B_r)}
\lesssim
\|\Omega\|_{L^3(B_{2r})}
+\text{cutoff terms}
\lesssim
C\eta r+	ext{cutoff terms}.
\]

Therefore large ambient strain in a genuinely low-vorticity buffer must be paid by one of

\[
\boxed{
\text{cutoff/Campanato boundary action}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{derivative roughness in the transition annulus}
\quad\lor\quad
S_{far}.
}
\]

If the buffer contains points with `|Omega|>2 eta`, then the ambient cell is adjacent to an active-vorticity cell and is routed to the active-buffer branch instead.

## 7. Far ambient strain is harmonic-affine

Inside `B_r`, the far velocity contribution is curl free and divergence free, hence

\[
h=\nabla\phi,
\qquad
\Delta\phi=0.
\]

Its strain is the harmonic Hessian

\[
S_{far}=\nabla^2\phi.
\]

Consequently a large ambient far strain is exactly a remote harmonic/affine action of the type audited in M5-291 and M5-309–M5-321.

It therefore routes to

\[
\boxed{
H_{harmonic}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{affine transition}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{screened affine shield}.
}
\]

The screened affine-shield saturation route has already been shown to produce an endpoint energy atom, and the atom routes back to `H_S,crit` by M5-330.

Thus the ambient branch does not create a new terminal mechanism.
