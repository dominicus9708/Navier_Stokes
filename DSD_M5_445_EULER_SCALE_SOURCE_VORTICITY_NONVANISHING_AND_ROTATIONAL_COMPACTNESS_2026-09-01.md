\[
R_j=K_jr_j,
\qquad

Use the canonical velocity scale

\[

=\frac{\nu R_j}{r_j^2}
=\frac{\nu K_j^2}{R_j}.

Then the source-scale vorticity unit is

\[
\frac{U_j^E}{R_j}
=\frac{\nu}{r_j^2}

Thus in source coordinates

\[

\[
\boxed{

## 2. Remote strain contribution becomes a fixed unit-annulus pairing

Let `A_j` be a dyadic-thickness source annulus at radius `R_j` carrying a fixed fraction of the target longitudinal strain.

After scaling by `R_j`, it becomes a fixed annulus

\[

\[

\[

\[
\boxed{

Therefore

\[
\boxed{

Fine-scale oscillation at internal scale `1/K_j` cannot erase the source in the weak limit because the source is detected by a fixed linear functional.
